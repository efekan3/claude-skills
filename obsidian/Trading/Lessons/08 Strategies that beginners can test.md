# 8. Strategies that beginners can test

*A strategy is a written set of rules: when to enter, when to exit, how much to risk. Here are four classic ones. Test them in the Strategy lab below.*

### 1. Dollar-cost averaging (DCA)

Buy a fixed amount every week or month, whatever the price. No timing needed and less stress. It suits long-term investing more than trading.

### 2. Trend following

Hold while the trend is up (e.g. EMA 50 above EMA 200), step aside when it turns down. You miss the exact bottom and top, but you aim to catch the big middle and avoid long bear markets.

### 3. Breakout

Buy when price closes above its highest level of the last 20 days, sell when it closes below the lowest of the last 10. It catches strong moves but has many small false starts.

### 4. Mean reversion (range trading)

Buy when RSI is very low (below 30) and sell when it recovers. It works in ranges and fails in strong downtrends, where “cheap” keeps getting cheaper.

### Pick one and keep a journal

Mixing strategies trade by trade is how plans fall apart. Write down why you entered every practice trade, then review.
