# 10. Your trading plan and leverage

*Put everything together in a plan you can follow, and understand why leverage is the fastest way to lose an account.*

### A one-page plan

- **Markets**: which coins and which timeframe.
- **Setup**: the exact condition you trade (e.g. trend up on daily + pullback to EMA 50 on 4h).
- **Risk**: max 1–2% per trade, max 3 open trades.
- **Exits**: stop under the last swing low; target at 2R or the next resistance.
- **Review**: weekly journal review. Stop trading for the week after losing 5% of the account.

### Leverage and futures

Leverage lets you trade with borrowed money. At 10× leverage, a 10% move against you wipes out the whole position (“liquidation”). Crypto moves 10% in a day regularly. Beginners should learn without leverage first. This app deliberately doesn't offer it.

### From practice to real money

Only consider real trades after a long stretch of practice where you followed your plan and the results held up. Start with small amounts you can afford to lose completely.

## Check yourself

**At 10× leverage, roughly what move against you liquidates the position?**
- 1%
- 10%
- 50%

> [!success]- Answer
> 10%. 10× magnifies a 10% move into a 100% loss of your margin.

**What belongs in a trading plan?**
- Only which coins to buy
- Setup, risk per trade, exits and a review routine
- Price predictions for next year

> [!success]- Answer
> Setup, risk per trade, exits and a review routine. A plan defines behaviour, not predictions.
