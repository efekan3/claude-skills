# 4. Reward-to-risk and win rate

*You don't have to be right most of the time. You need wins that are bigger than your losses.*

### Reward-to-risk (R:R)

If you risk €100 to make €200, your R:R is 2 : 1. Divide the distance to your target by the distance to your stop.

### Break-even win rate

- R:R 1 : 1 → you must win more than 50% of trades
- R:R 2 : 1 → about 34% is enough
- R:R 3 : 1 → about 25% is enough

This is why traders skip setups where the target is close and the stop is far away.

### Expectancy

Expectancy = (win rate × average win) − (loss rate × average loss). Positive expectancy over many trades is the real goal. One trade tells you nothing.

> [!example]
> 10 trades, 4 wins of €200, 6 losses of €100 → +€800 − €600 = **+€200**, despite losing 60% of the time.

## Check yourself

**With a 3 : 1 reward-to-risk, roughly what win rate breaks even?**
- 25%
- 50%
- 75%

> [!success]- Answer
> 25%. One win of 3 units pays for three losses of 1 unit.

**Which matters more over 100 trades?**
- Win rate alone
- Expectancy (wins × size vs. losses × size)
- The number of trades

> [!success]- Answer
> Expectancy (wins × size vs. losses × size). A high win rate with huge losses still loses money.
