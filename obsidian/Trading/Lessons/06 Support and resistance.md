# 6. Support and resistance

*Prices remember. Levels where many people bought or sold before often matter again.*

### Support

A price area where buyers stepped in before. Price often bounces there, until it doesn't. A clean break below support is a warning.

### Resistance

An area where sellers took control before. Breaking above it with strong volume can start a new move up.

### Flip

Broken resistance often becomes support (and the other way round). Traders who missed the breakout buy the retest.

### Practical tips

- Think in **zones**, not exact prices.
- The more touches and the higher the timeframe, the stronger the level.
- Stops just under support, targets just under resistance.

## Check yourself

**Price breaks above strong resistance and then comes back to it. What often happens?**
- The old resistance acts as support
- The level no longer matters
- Price always falls back into the range

> [!success]- Answer
> The old resistance acts as support. This is the “flip”. It doesn't always hold, so keep your stop.

**Where do you place targets relative to resistance?**
- Just above it
- Just below it
- Exactly on it

> [!success]- Answer
> Just below it. Others sell at resistance, so price may turn just before it.
