# 7. RSI, MACD and volume

*Indicators describe momentum. They are helpers for a plan, not signals to blindly follow.*

### RSI (Relative Strength Index)

Measures how strong recent up moves were compared with down moves, on a 0–100 scale. Above 70 is “overbought” and below 30 “oversold”. In a strong trend RSI can stay above 70 for weeks, so overbought is not a sell signal by itself.

### MACD

The gap between a fast and a slow moving average. When the MACD line crosses above its signal line, momentum is turning up. The histogram on the Chart tab shows that gap growing or shrinking.

### Volume

Moves on high volume are more trustworthy. A breakout on thin volume often fails.

### Divergence

Price makes a new high but RSI makes a lower high: momentum is fading. It's a warning, not a guarantee.

## Check yourself

**RSI is 78 during a strong uptrend. What does that mean?**
- Sell immediately
- Momentum is strong, the move may be stretched
- The trend has ended

> [!success]- Answer
> Momentum is strong, the move may be stretched. Overbought readings can last a long time in trends.

**A breakout on very low volume is…**
- More reliable
- Less reliable
- Unrelated to reliability

> [!success]- Answer
> Less reliable. Few participants means little conviction behind the move.
