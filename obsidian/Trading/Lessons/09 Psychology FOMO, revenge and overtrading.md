# 9. Psychology: FOMO, revenge and overtrading

*Most losses come from behaviour, not from bad analysis. Knowing the traps is half the protection.*

### FOMO (fear of missing out)

Buying because something already went up 30%. Late buyers often provide the exit liquidity for early ones. If you missed a move, wait for the next setup.

### Revenge trading

After a loss you immediately jump into another trade to “win it back”, usually bigger and without a plan. Take a break after a loss instead.

### Overtrading

Trading out of boredom. Fees add up and quality drops. Fewer, better trades almost always beat many average ones.

### Holding losers, selling winners

People hate realising a loss, so they hold losers and quickly sell winners. That's the opposite of what works. Let your stop and target decide.

### The trading journal

For each trade, write the reason, the plan (entry, stop, target), and afterwards what happened and how you felt. Patterns in your own behaviour show up fast.

## Check yourself

**You just took a loss. The healthiest next step is…**
- Double the size to win it back
- Pause and review the trade
- Remove your stop-losses

> [!success]- Answer
> Pause and review the trade. Emotional re-entries are the classic revenge trade.

**A coin is up 40% today and everyone is talking about it. A disciplined trader…**
- Buys immediately
- Waits for a setup that fits the plan
- Shorts it with leverage

> [!success]- Answer
> Waits for a setup that fits the plan. Chasing late is FOMO. Plans come first.
