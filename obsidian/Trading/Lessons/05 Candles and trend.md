# 5. Candles and trend

*Each candle tells you who won a period: buyers or sellers. Trend tells you which side has been winning over time.*

### Reading a candle

Green: closed higher than it opened. Red: closed lower. The thin **wicks** show the highest and lowest prices reached. A long wick means price went there but got pushed back.

### Trend by structure

- **Uptrend**: higher highs and higher lows.
- **Downtrend**: lower highs and lower lows.
- **Range**: price bouncing between the same high and low.

### Moving averages

The EMA 50 and EMA 200 on the Chart tab smooth price out. Price above a rising 200-day average is a classic sign of a long-term uptrend; below a falling one, a downtrend. Averages lag: they confirm trends, they don't predict them.

### Use more than one timeframe

Check the daily chart for the main trend, then a 1h or 4h chart for timing. Trading against the daily trend on a 5-minute chart is swimming against the current.

## Check yourself

**A series of higher highs and higher lows is…**
- A downtrend
- An uptrend
- A range

> [!success]- Answer
> An uptrend. Buyers keep pushing to new highs and defending higher lows.

**What's the main weakness of moving averages?**
- They lag behind price
- They only work on Bitcoin
- They change after the fact

> [!success]- Answer
> They lag behind price. They're built from past prices, so they confirm moves late.
