# Reward-to-risk

How much you aim to win compared with how much you risk. Target distance ÷ stop distance. At 2 : 1 you only need to win about 34% of trades to break even.

> [!example]
> Stop 5% below, target 10% above → reward-to-risk 2 : 1.
