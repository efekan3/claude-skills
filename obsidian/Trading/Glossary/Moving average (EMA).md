# Moving average (EMA)

The average price over a number of periods, drawn as a line. EMA gives more weight to recent prices. Price above a rising 200-day average is a classic sign of an uptrend.

> [!example]
> Turn on EMA 50 and EMA 200 on the 1D chart: when the 50 is above the 200, the trend is usually up.
