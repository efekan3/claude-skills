# Take-profit

An order that sells automatically when the price reaches your target, so you lock in a gain instead of hoping for more.

> [!example]
> Buy at €100 with a take-profit at €110: if price touches €110 the position closes with about +10% minus fees.

Learn more: [[03 Stop-loss and take-profit]]
