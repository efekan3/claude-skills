# RSI

The Relative Strength Index measures momentum from 0 to 100. Above 70 means the price rose fast (overbought), below 30 means it fell fast (oversold). In strong trends it can stay extreme for weeks, so it's a warning, not a signal.

> [!example]
> Switch the lower chart on the Chart tab to RSI to see it live.
