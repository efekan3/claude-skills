# MACD

The gap between a fast (12) and slow (26) moving average, with a signal line. When MACD crosses above its signal line, momentum is turning up; below, momentum is turning down.

> [!example]
> The bars under the chart (histogram) grow when momentum strengthens and shrink when it fades.
