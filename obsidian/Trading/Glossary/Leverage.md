# Leverage

Trading with borrowed money. At 10× leverage a 10% move against you wipes out the whole position (liquidation). Crypto moves 10% in a day regularly, so beginners should avoid it.

> [!example]
> With 10× leverage on €1,000.00, a 10% drop loses the full €1,000.00.
