# RSI mean reversion

**Rules:** Buy when the 14-day RSI drops below 30; sell when it recovers above 55.

- **Works best in:** Sideways ranges where price keeps bouncing between levels.
- **Struggles in:** Strong downtrends, where oversold keeps getting more oversold.
