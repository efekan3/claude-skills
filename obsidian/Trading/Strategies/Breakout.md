# 20-day breakout

**Rules:** Buy when the close beats the highest high of the last 20 days; sell when it closes under the lowest low of the last 10.

- **Works best in:** Markets that start strong moves after a quiet period.
- **Struggles in:** False breakouts that fall straight back into the range.
