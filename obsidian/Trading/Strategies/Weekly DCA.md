# Weekly DCA

**Rules:** Invest the same amount every week for the whole period, never sell. Compared with putting everything in on day one.

- **Works best in:** Long-term investing without timing the market.
- **Struggles in:** Doesn't protect you in a long bear market; it only spreads your entry price.
