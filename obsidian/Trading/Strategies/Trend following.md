# Trend following (EMA 50/200)

**Rules:** Hold while the 50-day average is above the 200-day average; sell when it crosses below.

- **Works best in:** Long, clear trends (bull or bear markets).
- **Struggles in:** Choppy sideways markets: many small losses from false crosses.
